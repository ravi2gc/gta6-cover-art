VICE GOLD is a golden-hour key-art studio: drop in your own photos and turn them into a GTA-style cover. A full-bleed hero close-up flagship with a cast strip, plus eight more multi-panel layouts, nine warm grades, face-aware crops that never cut eyes, mouth or chin, a hero pop-out cast strip, draggable callouts with leader lines, auto cast role tags, a billing block, a gold seal and heat stars — exported as PNG at 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080. Everything runs offline in the browser; photos never leave your device. Sample portraits (Sofia, Dante, Milo, Duo Gold) were made for this entry with an image model, plus the kit's boulevard and pier photos. Fonts are OFL (Oswald Bold for titles, Dancing Script for subtitles) and ship locally.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a browser. Pick a layout and a grade, click a photo into a panel, drag the photo to pan it, scroll to zoom, edit the title, and hit Export PNG.
